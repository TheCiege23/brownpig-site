The Headless division · In development

Fantasy sports, without the front end.

We built the engine that runs AllFantasy — real league connections, live rosters, scoring, the whole unglamorous middle layer that takes months to get right. Headless is that engine opened up, so somebody else can put their own product on top of it instead of building it again from scratch.

Where it's at

Being scoped right now.

We're not going to publish an API reference and a pricing table for something that isn't finished — that's exactly the kind of vapour this whole studio was built to avoid. What exists today is a working fantasy engine in production, serving a real product with real users, and a decision to open it up.

What it looks like from the outside — endpoints, licensing, whether it's an API or a full white-label build — is being worked out now. If you're the kind of person who'd use this, the most useful thing you can do is tell us what you'd need from it before we lock the shape. Early conversations are shaping it.

Who we think this is for

Early guesses, not promises.

01

Media and creators

An audience that already talks about fantasy every week, and no realistic way to build the platform underneath a league product of your own.

02

Apps that want a fantasy layer

Sportsbooks, communities, team apps — products where fantasy is a feature that should exist, not the reason the company exists.

03

Builders

You want to ship a fantasy product and you'd rather spend your time on what makes yours different than on parsing league data for six months.

If you're none of these and you still want it, that's genuinely useful to hear — it means we've drawn the box wrong.
