Headless is the part of Brown Pig with no name on it. Agencies, consultancies and studios bring us work their team doesn't have hours for, and it ships under their brand, to their client, with their name on the invoice. We never appear.

How it works

Quiet, by design.

01

NDA before anything else

We sign yours before you describe the client. If you'd rather anonymise the brief entirely, that works too — we've scoped plenty of projects that were only ever "a logistics company".

02

Fixed scope, priced to resell

You get a written scope and a fixed number so you can put your own margin on top and quote your client with confidence. No hourly meter to explain to anyone.

03

We work in your world

Your repo, your conventions, your ticket system, your staging environment. Commits under a neutral name if you want them that way. Whatever keeps it seamless on your side.

04

You're the only voice the client hears

We don't contact your client, don't appear in the credits, don't put it in this portfolio, and don't post about it. If you want us on a call, we join as your team.

05

Handover that survives us

Documented, deployable by your people, no hidden dependency on us. The point is that you could never call us again and nothing breaks.

Before you ask

The awkward ones.

Will you approach our client directly?

No, and it's in the agreement in writing with a non-solicit attached. Our own products are how we make money on the consumer side — poaching a partner's client would end the referral flow that makes this division worth running.

Can we mark it up?

Yes, and you should. We price the build to you; what you charge your client is your business and we don't want visibility into it.

What if our client wants to meet the developers?

We'll join the call as part of your team, using your branding, with whatever titles you've set up. Some partners prefer we never appear at all and relay everything — that's fine too.

Who owns the code?

You do, on delivery, with full IP assignment. Then you assign it onward to your client under your own contract. We keep nothing and reuse nothing that's specific to the project.

How many partners do you take?

Few. We're two people and we won't take a project we can't staff properly — if we're full we'll give you a date rather than say yes and slip it.
