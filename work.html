LiveB2CFamily

StoryVault

Your grandmother knows two hundred stories and nobody has written down one. StoryVault is a private family archive: you start it, text your relatives a link, and one question a week fills it in — typed or in their own recorded voice. Nothing public, nothing sold. This is the one we built out of losing something.

Under the hood

What these are built on.

Same spine across the shelf, which is why a new product takes weeks instead of quarters. Next.js and React on the front, Postgres behind it, Stripe for money, Resend for mail, Twilio for messaging, deployed on Railway with the domains on Cloudflare.

Nothing here is exotic on purpose. Boring infrastructure is what lets two people run six products without a pager rotation — and it's what makes handing a build over to your team painless when we do client work.
