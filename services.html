Where Headless is at

How we price and work

No hourly meter. No surprise invoice.

Fixed scope, fixed price

After the teardown you get a written scope with a number and a date attached. What's in, what's explicitly out, what it costs. If we got the estimate wrong, that's our problem — the price is the price.

You own everything

The repo, the code, the domain, the hosting accounts, the Stripe keys. Set up in your name from day one. We'll keep running it if you want, but you can walk away with the whole thing at any point. That's deliberate.

A link in week one

You don't get a status report, you get a URL you can click and poke at. Then it gets better every week in front of you. Nobody should have to take a build on faith for two months.

We'll say no

If your problem is solved by an off-the-shelf tool, or the budget doesn't match the build, we'll tell you on the first call. A bad-fit project is worse for us than no project.

Questions we get

Straight answers.

How fast can you actually ship?

A focused internal tool is usually 2–4 weeks. A consumer app with accounts and billing is 6–10. We work AI-native, which genuinely compresses the build — but it doesn't compress deciding what to build, and that's the part that sets the date.

Are you a big agency?

No. We're two people and we're upfront about it. That means you talk to the person building it, and it means we take a small number of projects at once. If we're full, we'll say so rather than queue you quietly.

"AI-native" — does a robot write my software?

No. It means the person who diagnoses your problem and designs the fix also directs the build, instead of writing a spec and throwing it over a wall. Everything gets reviewed, tested and deployed by us, and we're on the hook for it either way.

What if we already have a developer?

Fine. We've slotted alongside in-house teams and picked up half-finished work. We can take one contained piece, or audit what exists and hand back a plan you give to your own people. No requirement to use us for the build.

What does it cost?

Small tools start in the low four figures; full consumer apps with billing run considerably more. We won't pretend a number is meaningful before we know the problem — the teardown call is free and ends with a real figure.

Do you do maintenance afterwards?

If you want it. A monthly retainer covers hosting, monitoring, dependency updates and a bucket of change requests. If you'd rather run it yourself, we hand over documentation and stay reachable.
